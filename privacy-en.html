Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 20, 2026

1. Data Collection

Duas and Surahs values user privacy. The app collects the following data:

  • Location data: Used for prayer times, qibla direction, and nearby mosque features. Location data is not stored on servers.
  • Usage analytics: Anonymous usage statistics are collected through Firebase Analytics (screen views, feature usage).
  • Advertising data: Personalized ads are shown through Google AdMob. AdMob's data collection practices are subject to Google's privacy policy.

2. Use of Data

Collected data is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Calculating prayer times and qibla direction
  • Showing nearby mosques
  • Improving app experience
  • Ad serving

3. Data Storage

User preferences (favorite verses, reading progress, settings) are stored locally on the device only (SharedPreferences). This data is not sent to servers.

4. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Analytics: Anonymous usage statistics
  • Google AdMob: Ad serving
  • Aladhan API: Prayer times data
  • OpenStreetMap: Nearby mosque data

5. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

6. Changes

This privacy policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be published on this page.
